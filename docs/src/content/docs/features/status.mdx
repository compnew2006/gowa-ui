## Overview

WhatsApp Status lets you post a text, image, or video story that your contacts
see for 24 hours. In Gowa-UI, posting a status does not leave the chat view —
it lives in a dedicated **Status** conversation pinned at the top of the
sidebar, so you can publish a story as easily as sending a message.

## Key Features

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="In the Sidebar" icon="rocket">
    The Status conversation is always pinned at the top of the chat list, on
    every tab. No separate page to navigate to.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Text, Image & Video" icon="set-logo">
    Post a plain text story, or attach an image or video with an optional
    caption — the same composer you already use for messaging.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Any Account" icon="setting">
    When your organization has more than one connected WhatsApp account, pick
    which one the story posts from.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Send-Only" icon="approve-check">
    The conversation shows a session log of what you posted. Inbound status
    views/replies are not surfaced here.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Posting a Status

<Steps>

1. **Open the Status conversation**

   Open the **Status** conversation at the top of the sidebar.

2. **Pick the account (if you have several)**

   When your organization has more than one connected WhatsApp account, select
   the one to post from in the account tab strip.

3. **Compose your story**

   Type your text, or attach an image/video using the paperclip button. Add an
   optional caption for media.

4. **Send**

   Press **Send**. The story is published and appears in the conversation log.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="Where does the status go?">
  A status is addressed to WhatsApp's well-known `status@broadcast` JID. It is
  **not** a 1:1 conversation: posting one does not create a contact or a
  message in your history. The dashboard keeps only a session-local log of
  what you posted, so it clears on refresh.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="Account must be connected">
  The story is published through the selected account's GOWA connection. If
  that account is disconnected, the post fails — reconnect it under
  **WhatsApp Accounts** first.
</Aside>
