# Whatomate

## Users
Business owners, customer support teams, and sales agents who manage WhatsApp conversations at scale. They range from solo operators to teams of 50+ agents in a single organization. They use the platform daily for hours at a time, often switching between multiple conversations rapidly.

## Product Purpose
Open-source WhatsApp Business API platform. Multi-agent inbox with conversation assignment, real-time messaging via WhatsApp Cloud API, contact management, and team collaboration tools. The /chat route is the primary work surface where agents spend most of their time.

## Brand Personality
Professional but approachable. Efficient over flashy. The product earns trust through reliability and speed, not visual spectacle. Conversational tone in UI copy, never corporate.

## Register
product

## Tone
Clear, direct, human. Short sentences. No jargon. Error messages explain what happened and what to do next.

## Anti-references
- Intercom-style heavy branding and illustrations
- Salesforce-style dense enterprise UI
- Generic SaaS dashboard templates with hero metrics

## Strategic Principles
1. Speed of interaction: every click and keystroke matters
2. Information density without clutter
3. Multi-theme support is a core feature, not an afterthought
4. RTL (Arabic) is a first-class citizen
5. Accessible by default: WCAG 2.1 AA target
